To My Godparent to Be

I’m still too little to write this on my own, so Mommy and Daddy are helping me.

They’ve been thinking and praying about the people who will help guide me as I grow in faith, kindness, and love.

When they thought of someone special…

They thought of you.

My Mommy and Daddy chose you because they believe you’ll be someone I can look up to not only today, but for many years to come.

So before I learn my first words…

Before I take my first steps…

I’d love to ask you my very first big question…

Will you do me the honor of being my Ninong/Ninang?

With tiny hugs and lots of love,

Maria Zeleora 🤍
